# Hobsonville Ultrasound

## Purpose
A static Astro website for a local diagnostic ultrasound clinic in Hobsonville, Auckland. Help patients understand services, preparation and pricing, then book through the existing TidyCal system. Help referrers contact the clinic. The primary audience includes older patients, pregnant patients, people attending general and musculoskeletal scans, and referring health professionals.

## Source and scope
Based on the client's September 2026 email agreement, presentation, content brief, pricing PDF, appointment policy and supplied photography. Five categories: pregnancy, general, musculoskeletal, small parts and vascular. Pregnancy, pelvic and abdominal scans have prominent routes. No paediatric category. Formerly Body 2 Baby Ultrasound; same ownership, team and diagnostic focus.

Build locally, check, then publish source to a private GitHub repository. Production deployment is a separate step. Clinical copy, prices, eligibility and uncertain availability need client approval before public launch. No patient-data forms, invented testimonials or invented credentials. Use real clinic photographs and licensed illustrative stock.

## Success
Clear booking and phone actions, readable service and preparation information, transparent prices, simple directions, accessible navigation, responsive layouts and fast static output. Retain existing main URLs. Document launch dependencies without exposing editorial notes in patient-facing pages.
