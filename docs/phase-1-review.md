# Phase 1: service and clinic-information review

Status: local review only. Phase 2 must wait for the user's explicit approval.

## Implemented

- Weekday opening hours: Monday–Friday, 9:30 am–5:00 pm. The old Saturday hours are omitted; the site does not claim Saturday closure.
- No patient charge for examinations with an ACC referral, consistently across pricing, FAQs, referrers and musculoskeletal information.
- General examination duration: approximately 20–30 minutes, with the existing qualification that duration varies by examination.
- Shoulder clothing: singlet, camisole or loose-fitting top allowing access to the shoulder and upper arm. Other joints retain accessible-clothing guidance.
- Gender, combined growth + 3D/4D, anatomy and standalone 3D/4D are excluded from available-service directories, booking choices, prices, related links and FAQ service lists.
- Existing URLs for these four scans display only an unavailable-service notice and reception contact within the shared site shell. They have no scan price, scan booking action or Service schema, are marked noindex, and are excluded from the sitemap.
- The local draft displays $300 for diagnostic pregnancy scans for patients not eligible for NZ maternity funding. This interpretation requires confirmation before publication.
